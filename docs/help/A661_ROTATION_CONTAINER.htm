A661_ROTATION_CONTAINER (RotationContainer)

Categories: Container

A RotationContainer widget applies a rotation transformation to a group of widgets. Widgets placed within RotationContainer have their coordinates referenced to the first ancestor with a PosX, PosY reference point.

Restriction: For RotationContainer restrictions refer to Table 3.2.3.1 for children/parents.

ParameterChangeDescription
WidgetIdentDUnique identifier of the widget.
ParentIdentDIdentifier of the immediate container of the widget.
VisibleDRVisibility of the widget
CenterXDRX position of the center of the rotation
CenterYDRY position of the center of the rotation
RotationAngleDRRotation angle to be applied to the children widgets

A661_GP_TRIANGLE_FAN (GpTriangleFan)

Categories: Graphical Representation

The graphical primitive GpTriangleFan widget enables the definition of a shape composed out of a fan of triangles. The first three vertices define the first triangular section. Each subsequent vertex defines a new triangular section, sharing the first and last vertices of the previous triangular section. At least three vertices must be specified. Any convex polygon can be represented as a triangle fan, by just specifying its vertices in the natural order. A triangle fan is not necessarily a convex polygon, though.
